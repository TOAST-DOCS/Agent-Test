## Compute > Instance > Pricing guide { #compute-instance-pricing-guide }

This document outlines instance pricing plans and the service limits provided for each plan. Pricing may vary depending on the region and billing method. You can find detailed pricing information in the console's pricing calculator. (Body modification test: This sentence should be reflected when the translation is re-executed.)

<a id="plan-limits"></a>
### Limits by plan { #plan-limits }

| Plan | Max instances | Max block storage | Technical support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic plan | 10 | 1TB | Email support |
| Standard plan | 50 | 10TB | Email and phone support |
| Premium plan | 200 | 50TB | Dedicated engineer support |

<a id="billing-notes"></a>
### Billing notes { #billing-notes }

Billing is calculated hourly, and storage charges continue to accrue even when an instance is stopped. If you use a monthly-cap plan, any usage above the cap is not billed.
